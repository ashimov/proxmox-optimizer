# Code of Conduct

Short version: be decent to people.

## What is expected

- Be respectful and constructive. Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other side is competent and acting in good faith until shown otherwise.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no to an idea that is
  perfectly reasonable.
- Keep discussion on the technical question at hand.

## What is not

- Personal attacks, insults or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, in the open or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Deliberately derailing discussions or issue threads.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space
where you are taking part in the project.

## Reporting

Email <berik@ashimov.com>. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit, delete or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that do not fit the above, and may block accounts for repeated or
severe behaviour.
